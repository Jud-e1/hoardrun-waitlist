FAQ

Questions, answered.

What is Hoardrun?

Hoardrun is a financial technology company, not a bank. We bring saving, spending, sending and investing together in one app, with low costs and clear information so you can decide for yourself.

Does joining the waitlist cost anything?

No. Joining the waitlist is completely free, and there's no obligation to open an account when your invite arrives.

When will I get access?

We're opening access in waves. Waitlist members are invited first, in the order they signed up. We'll email you when your spot is ready.

What do you do with my information?

We only use your details to manage your waitlist spot and send you launch updates. We don't sell your data, and you can unsubscribe any time.

Can I use Hoardrun on my computer?

Yes. Hoardrun works on your phone or your computer, wherever you are.
